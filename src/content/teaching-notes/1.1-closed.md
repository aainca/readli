# 1.1 Closed (short vowel)

## Rule
A vowel closed in by a consonant says its short sound: a as in cat, i as in pin.

## How to teach
1. Write "cat". Cover the t with your finger and say "the a is trapped by the t, so it stays short."
2. Read cat, map, pen, sun together, tapping each sound.
3. Let him read the next five alone.

## What to say
"Is there a consonant after the vowel? Then the vowel stays short."
"Tap it out: c, a, t."

## Common mistakes
He says the long sound (cape for cap). Do not say the word. Point to the last consonant and ask what is trapping the vowel.
He guesses from the first letter. Ask him to tap every sound.

## Activity
Write a, e, i, o, u on sticky notes. Say a short vowel sound; he grabs the right note and names a word.

## Ready when
He reads new short vowel words on the first try without tapping.
