# 1.3 Silent e

## Rule
An e at the end of a word is silent and makes the vowel before it say its name.

## How to teach
1. Write "cap". Have him read it.
2. Add an e and say "the e is quiet, but it makes the a say its name." Read "cape" together.
3. Repeat with pin and pine, hop and hope. Then let him do the next pair alone.

## What to say
"Is there an e at the end? Then what does the vowel say?"
"Silent e, long vowel."

## Common mistakes
He reads "cape" as "cap". Do not say the word. Point to the e and ask the question above.
He says the e out loud (cap-ee). Remind him the e is quiet.

## Activity
Write 5 short words on paper (tap, kit, not, cub, man). He adds an e to each and reads both.

## Ready when
He reads new silent e words correctly on the first try without pointing to the e.
