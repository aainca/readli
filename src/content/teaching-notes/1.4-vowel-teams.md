# 1.4 Vowel teams

## Rule
Two vowels together make one sound, usually the first one's name: rain, feet, boat.

## How to teach
1. Write "rain". Circle "ai" and say "these two work as a team. The first one talks, the second one is quiet."
2. Read rain, pain, mail together, then boat, coat, road. Keep one team per round.
3. Let him read five new words alone before switching teams.

## What to say
"Find the team. Which vowel is first? That is the sound."
"When two vowels go walking, the first one does the talking."

## Common mistakes
He reads only the first vowel as short (ran for rain). Ask him to find the team and circle it.
He says both vowels. A team makes one sound.

## Activity
Write ai, ee, oa on paper. He hunts for words around the house (cereal box, mail) that use each team.

## Ready when
He spots the team before reading and gets new team words right on the first try.
