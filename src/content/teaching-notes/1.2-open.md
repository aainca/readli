# 1.2 Open (long vowel)

## Rule
A syllable that ends in a vowel is open, so the vowel says its name: go, me, ro-bot.

## How to teach
1. Write "go". Point to the end and say "nothing comes after the o, so it can say its name."
2. Compare "got" and "go". Cover the t and watch the vowel change.
3. Read no, so, hi, we together, then he tries she, fly, my.

## What to say
"What is at the end of the syllable? A vowel? Then it says its name."
"Open door, long sound."

## Common mistakes
He reads "go" as "gah". Ask what comes after the o. Nothing, so it is open.
In ro-bot he closes the first part. Cover "bot" and read "ro" alone.

## Activity
Write five words on paper: not, hop, bit, met, cut. He erases the last letter and reads the open word that is left.

## Ready when
He reads new open syllables with the long sound without stopping to think.
