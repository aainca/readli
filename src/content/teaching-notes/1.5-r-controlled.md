# 1.5 R-controlled (bossy r)

## Rule
An r right after a vowel changes the vowel's sound: car, fork, her, bird, turn.

## How to teach
1. Write "cat" and "car". Read both. Say "the r is bossy. It changes the a."
2. Practice ar words, then or words. Save er, ir, ur for later; they sound the same.
3. Let him read five new words with one r sound alone.

## What to say
"Is there an r after the vowel? Then the r is the boss."
"Say the vowel and the r together, as one sound."

## Common mistakes
He reads the vowel short and adds r (cuh-ar). Ask for the vowel and r as one chunk.
He mixes up er, ir, ur. That is fine; they sound the same.

## Activity
Write ar, or, er, ir, ur on five cards. Say a word (farm, corn, bird). He holds up the card that matches.

## Ready when
He reads new car, corn and bird-type words smoothly on the first try.
