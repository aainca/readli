# 1.6 Consonant-le

## Rule
A consonant plus le at the end makes its own last syllable: ta-ble, can-dle.

## How to teach
1. Write "table". Count back three letters from the end and draw a line: ta | ble.
2. Say "ble says bul. Now read the first part." It is open (ta) if it ends with a vowel, closed (can) if not.
3. Do candle, puzzle, apple together, then let him split and read two alone.

## What to say
"Count back three. Draw the line. Read the first part, then add the ending."

## Common mistakes
He says the e (tab-lee). Remind him the ending just says bul, dul, zul.
He reads the first part wrong. Cover the ending and ask if the first part is open or closed.

## Activity
Write table, candle, apple, puzzle, turtle on paper. He draws each split line and reads them.

## Ready when
He splits new consonant-le words himself and reads them on the first try.
