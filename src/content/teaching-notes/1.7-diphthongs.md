# 1.7 Diphthongs

## Rule
Some vowel pairs make a gliding sound: oi and oy as in coin and boy, ou and ow as in out and cow.

## How to teach
1. Write "boy". Say it slowly so he feels his mouth move. "Two vowels, one sliding sound."
2. Practice oi and oy together (coin, boy, toy, join), then ou and ow (out, cow, loud, town).
3. Let him read five new words alone.

## What to say
"Find the pair. Does your mouth slide? That is the gliding sound."

## Common mistakes
He reads ow as in snow instead of cow. Have him try the other sound and pick the real word.
He reads only one vowel. Ask him to find the pair first.

## Activity
Say a word. He answers "oi" or "ow" and points left or right. Mix in plain words (cat) he should not point for.

## Ready when
He reads new oi, oy, ou and ow words correctly on the first try.
